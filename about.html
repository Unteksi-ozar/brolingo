Mission & Vision
What We Do (At Scale)

Brolingo is an AI-forward, agentically aligned lexicographic platform delivering next-generation corporate vocabulary intelligence to a global community of knowledge workers, individual contributors, and professional humans operating within enterprise ecosystems.

We leverage cutting-edge dictionary technology to surface key learnings from an innovative, localized talent community spanning multiple time zones, verticals, and Slack workspaces. Our core competency is helping you understand what people are saying in meetings when they are not, technically, saying anything.

Official Position Statement
We don't just define words. We ideate around linguistic paradigm shifts, productize semantic insights, and circle back on terminology that has long since lost all meaning.

At its heart, Brolingo exists because someone in a meeting said "let's double-click on the learnings from our synergy-driven flywheel" and we needed somewhere to process that.

Team & Culture
Our People (Headcount TBD)

Brolingo is powered by a lean, agile, cross-functional team of passionate stakeholders operating at the intersection of satire and mild workplace trauma. We are remote-first (extremely remote), asynchronously aligned (no meetings), and fully committed to our north star (making fun of the meetings we no longer attend).

Our talent density is high. Our org chart is flat. Our headcount is not something we're disclosing at this stage of the journey, but we can confirm it rhymes with "fun."

We believe in psychological safety, radical candour, and bringing your whole self to work, provided your whole self has reviewed the Brolingo glossary and knows what a deliverable is.
